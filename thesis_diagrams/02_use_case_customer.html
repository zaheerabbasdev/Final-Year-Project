Use Case Diagram — Customer Actor

Kaarkun Service Marketplace Platform

Customer Use Case Descriptions

#Use CaseDescription
UC1Register AccountCustomer signs up with name, email, phone and password. An OTP is sent to verify the email address.
UC2LoginAuthenticated access using email and password; JWT token issued on success.
UC3Forgot / Reset PasswordCustomer requests a 6-digit OTP via email, then sets a new password.
UC4Update ProfileEdit full name, phone number and location details.
UC5Upload Profile PhotoChoose and upload an avatar image stored on AWS S3.
UC6Post a JobCreate a service request with title, description, category, budget, location and urgency flag.
UC7View My JobsList all job postings created by the customer with their current status.
UC8Browse Service ProvidersDiscover registered service providers on the platform.
UC9View Provider ProfileInspect a provider's experience, skills, availability, location and reviews.
UC10View Bids on JobSee all bids submitted by providers for a specific job posting.
UC11Accept a BidSelect a provider's bid; a booking record is automatically created. «include» UC12.
UC12View My BookingsList all active and historical bookings with status tracking.
UC13Complete BookingMark a booking as finished once the service is delivered. «include» UC14.
UC14Submit Review & RatingWrite a review and assign a star rating to the service provider.
UC15Chat with ProviderExchange real-time text and image messages with the assigned provider via WebSocket.
