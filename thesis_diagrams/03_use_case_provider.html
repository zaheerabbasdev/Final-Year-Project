Use Case Diagram — Service Provider Actor

Kaarkun Service Marketplace Platform

Service Provider Use Case Descriptions

#Use CaseDescription
UC1Register AccountProvider signs up with name, email, phone, password, experience years and service category. Includes uploading identity documents. «include» UC2.
UC2Upload CNIC & CertificatesSubmit national identity card image and professional qualification documents to AWS S3 during registration.
UC3LoginAuthenticated access using email and password; JWT token issued on success.
UC4Forgot / Reset PasswordProvider requests a 6-digit OTP via email, then sets a new password.
UC5Update Profile & SkillsEdit name, phone, bio, skill tags and geographical location.
UC6Set AvailabilityToggle the availability flag so customers see whether the provider is open for new work.
UC7View Dashboard & EarningsAccess a summary of total earnings, total bookings, active bids and average star rating.
UC8Browse Available JobsDiscover open job postings, filterable by service category and location.
UC9View Job DetailsInspect a job's description, budget, urgency, customer location and any existing bids.
UC10Place a BidSubmit a price quote and covering message for a job. «include» UC11.
UC11View My BidsList all submitted bids with their statuses (pending, accepted, rejected).
UC12Accept BookingConfirm a booking that was auto-created when the customer accepted the provider's bid. «include» UC13.
UC13Start & Complete ServiceMark a booking as in-progress on arrival at the job site, then mark it complete when the work is done.
UC14View My Reviews & RatingRead customer feedback and see the aggregated star rating across all completed bookings.
UC15Chat with CustomerExchange real-time text and image messages with the booking customer via WebSocket.
